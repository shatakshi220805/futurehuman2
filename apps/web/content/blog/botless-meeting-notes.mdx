---
title: Why we never send a bot to your calls
excerpt: Notes from system audio, not a guest in the lobby.
date: 2026-10-01
author: Futurehuman
---

A notetaker bot changes the conversation. People go quiet. Legal asks questions. The lobby fills with names nobody invited.

Futurehuman captures on-device: system audio and microphone, streamed to speech-to-text, diarized, then summarized. After the call you get an executive summary, action items with owners, and a Gmail draft. We never auto-send.

Consent reminders follow the jurisdiction you set. A meeting can be deleted on its own, fully.
