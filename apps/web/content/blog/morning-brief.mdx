The default routine runs weekdays at 7:00 AM in your timezone.

It scans overnight mail, Slack, calendar, and the client priorities in memory, then writes three numbered actions. You can deliver it in the app, email, or iMessage.

Routines are capped on Basic (5) and Plus (100). The job runner retries failed runs and keeps a history you can audit.
