Futurehuman is the assistant that understands your work — not because you pasted a brief, but because it was in the room, on the screen, and in the apps you already use.

No bot joins your calls. Notes are written after you hang up. Chat answers cite the originating item. You can pause or hard-delete anything it kept.

This is the product we wanted on Monday morning: three priorities, the deck in front of us, and no tab archaeology.
